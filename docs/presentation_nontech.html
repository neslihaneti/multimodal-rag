THE BUSINESS CHALLENGE

Why Standard Enterprise Search & ChatGPT Fail

Over 40% of critical corporate knowledge is trapped inside visual diagrams, schematics, and tables.

THE PROBLEM
❌ The 4 Big Risks of Standard AI Search
  • Blind to Visuals: Standard search tools strip away pictures, blueprints, and charts. If a key fact is in a workflow diagram, standard AI can't see it.
  • Dangerous "Confident" Hallucinations: When standard AI doesn't know the answer, it frequently fabricates believable but incorrect facts.
  • Data Privacy & Legal Liability: Sending proprietary trade secrets, financial contracts, or patents to third-party cloud servers risks IP leaks and regulatory fines.
  • Uncapped Recurring Cloud Bills: Paying per question or per token creates an unpredictable operational cost that grows month over month.

The Result: Employees waste 20% of their working hours manually double-checking whether AI answers are accurate.

💼 Real-World Enterprise Impact
  • Engineering & Operations: Technicians need exact mirror angles or component tolerances from manuals. A wrong number causes physical failure.
  • Legal & Compliance: Contracts contain nested clauses and signature blocks. Misattributing a clause can lead to contractual breach.
  • Finance & Auditing: Financial statements use complex multi-column tables. Text-only tools scramble columns and corrupt numbers.
  • Total In-House Control: Company data stays safely on corporate premises behind corporate firewalls.

Our Mission: Give employees instantaneous, truthful answers backed by visual evidence they can click and verify in 2 seconds.

Key Problem: Text-only search cannot solve visual document intelligence
